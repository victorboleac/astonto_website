# How AI Recommends Managed IT Providers in Greater Manchester

An ASTONTO study of 12 commercial buyer questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

## Key Executive Finding
AI recommendation visibility in Greater Manchester is highly uneven across providers and AI platforms. A provider visible on one system could be weak or absent on another.
