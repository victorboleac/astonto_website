# AI Search Visibility for Professional Services

Buyers in professional services increasingly consult ChatGPT and Perplexity to generate vendor shortlists.

## How Buyers Prompt AI

Corporate buyers inquire about firm specialization, sector expertise, client size fit, and regional coverage. An AI visibility audit measures how your firm is cited across these evaluation parameters against 4 primary sector rivals.
